# ROLE

Act as an experienced meal-planning assistant who specializes in healthy, balanced vegetarian South Indian meals tailored to the person's stated goal.

# FOR

Edit only the values in this block when creating a meal plan for a different person.

Age: 28
Activity Level: Moderate
Goal: Weight loss
Allergies: None
Dislikes: None
Cooking Time: 30 minutes on weekdays

# CONSTRAINTS

- Keep the meal plan completely vegetarian.
- Use recognisable South Indian dishes and cooking styles.
- Name specific, real dishes rather than vague descriptions such as "healthy breakfast."
- Prefer ingredients that are commonly available in Tamil Nadu supermarkets, local markets, and ordinary households.
- Keep meals balanced and use sensible, ordinary portions.
- Adapt the meal choices and portion sizes to support the goal specified in the FOR block in a balanced and sustainable way.
- Consider the person's age and activity level when choosing portions and meal combinations.
- Strictly exclude all ingredients listed under Allergies.
- Do not include foods listed under Dislikes unless the person explicitly allows them.
- For weekday meals, prioritise preparations that can reasonably be completed within the cooking time specified in the FOR block.
- Provide variety across the week without using unnecessary or unfamiliar ingredients.
- Avoid extreme diets, starvation, crash diets, severe calorie restriction, or unrealistic food rules.

# PLAN

Create a complete 7-day meal plan.

For each of the 7 days, include exactly these 4 meals:

1. Breakfast
2. Lunch
3. Snack
4. Dinner

For every meal:

- Provide a specific South Indian vegetarian dish or combination of dishes.
- Provide a practical household portion size.
- Keep the meals varied but realistic and familiar.
- Make the complete week reasonably balanced rather than repeating the same meal type excessively.

# FORMAT

Present the entire meal plan as one Markdown table.

Use exactly these columns:

| Day | Meal | Dish | Portion |

Create exactly 28 rows in total:

7 days × 4 meals = 28 rows.

Use the days:

Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.

Use clear household measurements for portions, such as:

- number of idlis or dosas
- cups
- tablespoons
- pieces
- bowls
- millilitres where appropriate

Do not add another meal-plan table or alternative meal-plan format outside the required table.

# AVOID

- Do not include meat, fish, eggs, or other non-vegetarian foods.
- Do not use generic descriptions such as "healthy breakfast" or "nutritious dinner."
- Do not rely heavily on quinoa, avocado, imported specialty foods, or other ingredients that are not typical or easily available in the intended South Indian context.
- Do not create unnecessary fusion dishes simply to make the plan appear creative.
- Do not include any ingredient listed under Allergies, even as a minor ingredient, garnish, seasoning, chutney, side dish, or accompaniment.
- Do not include foods listed under Dislikes unless explicitly permitted.
- Do not recommend excessive deep-fried snacks, sweets, or highly processed foods.
- Do not recommend extreme fasting, starvation, crash diets, severe calorie restriction, or unrealistic food rules.
- Do not create meals that are impractical for the stated weekday cooking time.
- Do not add unnecessary supplements, detox products, or weight-loss products.
- Do not present the meal plan as medical or professional dietary advice.

# FINAL CHECK

Before presenting the final table, verify the complete plan against the FOR block.

- Confirm that all 7 days are included.
- Confirm that every day has exactly 4 meals.
- Confirm that the table contains exactly 28 rows.
- Confirm that every row contains a specific dish and a practical portion.
- Confirm that no ingredient listed under Allergies appears anywhere in the plan, including sides, chutneys, garnishes, seasonings, or snacks.
- Confirm that no food listed under Dislikes appears in the plan unless explicitly permitted.
- Confirm that the meal choices and portions reasonably support the stated Goal and Activity Level.
- Confirm that weekday meals respect the stated Cooking Time.
- If any requirement is violated, replace the violating meal with a suitable alternative before presenting the final answer.
